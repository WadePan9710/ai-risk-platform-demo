# Expense and Travel Policy

## 报销与差旅政策

差旅和客户会议费用需要提前申请。超过 5,000 THB 的费用应在预订前提交申请。
报销时必须附上发票和业务目的；直属经理先审批，财务做最终审核。
没有有效发票的费用，需要财务书面批准例外。

> Updated: 2026-10-20
> Scope: business travel and client meetings

## Reimbursement process

1. Submit the request before booking when the amount exceeds 5,000 THB.
2. Attach receipts and the business purpose.
3. The line manager approves first; Finance performs the final review.
4. Reimbursement is normally paid in the next payroll cycle.

## Receipt requirement

An expense without a valid receipt requires a written exception approved by Finance.
